Your own order for claim entries, one fare for a return, Event purchases, and an
About page.

## New

- **Arrange entries.** On a tour or its claim report, press **Arrange** and drag
  entries into the order you want under each heading. The report, the spreadsheet
  and the bill picture numbers follow it. Entries you add later go by their date, and
  **Back to date order** undoes it.
- **One fare for both ways.** On a transport entry, switch on **Return journey too**.
  Choose **One for both ways** when a single fare covers the round trip: it counts
  once and reads as Home → Station → Home. Choose **Separate fares** for two entries,
  as before.
- **Event purchases** is a category of its own, with its own heading on a claim, for
  things bought for the event. A note such as `chocolates for the event` picks it.
  Entries you filed elsewhere stay there until you change them.
- **Bill captions say what you bought**, including on a claim that gives only totals
  by category.
- **About.** **More → About** says what the app is, who makes it and where its source
  is.

## If you already added a return as a second entry

To make one fare cover both: delete the second entry, then open the first, switch on
**Return journey too** and keep **One for both ways**.

## Update your other devices too

Editing a tour on a device with an older version puts that tour back in date order,
and editing a round-trip entry there removes its round-trip mark. Until it updates,
an older version shows Event purchases as "Other", under Miscellaneous, and a
round-trip entry as one way: do not add a return to it there.

## Fixed

## Updating

If you have 0.1.2 or later, the app offers this update by itself (**More → Updates**
to check now). From 0.1.1 or earlier, install it by hand once. Your entries stay.

## Which file

| Windows on ARM | `…_arm64-setup.exe` |
| Debian, Ubuntu | `…_amd64.deb` or `…_arm64.deb` |

| Other Linux (64-bit PC) | `…_amd64.AppImage` |
| Android 8 or later (ARM phones and tablets) | `…_android.apk` |

`latest.json` and `…app.tar.gz` are what installed apps use to update; you do not
need them.

## Security warnings you will see

The desktop apps are not signed with a paid developer certificate.

- **macOS:** open the app once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and
  choose **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows, SmartScreen warning:** choose **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Windows, Smart App Control:** where it is switched on, Windows blocks the
  installer, including during an update.
- **Android:** allow your browser, file manager or TourLedger to install apps.
